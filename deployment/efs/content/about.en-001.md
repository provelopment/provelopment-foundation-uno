# About this starter

Foundation Uno is a neutral, independently deployable website foundation. This sample installation is an illustrative placeholder rather than a real organisation.

## What this sample demonstrates

- One Entity owns one selected public Site.
- Pages declare their own language editions instead of inheriting a guess.
- Authored content is validated before publication, and a missing or invalid source is refused rather than replaced.

## What it does not claim

This starter is not a finished business website, a hosted service or a promise of delivery. Richer modules, accessible interaction patterns and installation workflows remain separately reviewed work.
