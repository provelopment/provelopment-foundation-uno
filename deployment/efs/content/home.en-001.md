# Foundation Uno

One organisation. One connected digital presence.

Foundation Uno is an independent, Apache-2.0 Next.js and TypeScript website foundation designed to help a single organisation manage its websites, pages and regional editions with clear ownership.

## One organisation, clear structure

A Foundation Uno installation is designed around one Entity. Facets organise areas of its work, Sites give those areas their own experiences, and Pages contain explicitly authored language-and-region editions. Each Page belongs to one Site and each Site belongs to one Facet.

## Deliberate publishing

A Site is selected explicitly rather than inferred from a hostname or a visitor's location. Page content is declared and validated before it can be published. Regional editions use complete locale identifiers such as `en-001`; missing translations are not invented.

## Open foundation

The project is intended to remain independently deployable and adaptable. Website modules, publishing, accessibility, safe actions and installation workflows are developed and reviewed as separate capabilities. An authored source file alone does not make a public Page.
