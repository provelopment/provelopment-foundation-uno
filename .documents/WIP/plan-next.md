## One unissued candidate

**Owner release decision (candidate only, not issued).** U03's publication engine and its proof are complete locally: a
synthetic declaration makes the real production build serve published Home and About Pages in two authored locales with
truthful `<html lang>`, canonical, same-Page `hreflang`, one `x-default` on the default Home, a temporary `/en` redirect,
and a `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` that agree with the HTML, while draft, unlisted, unknown and malformed addresses
stay real 404s. That proof is now ownership-safe: exclusive creation is the only claim, device/inode identity is the only
proof for removal, a stranger's file is never deleted, a replaced file fails closed with its recovery copies kept, and
containment is component-aware. The committed declaration still states no base and publishes no Page, so the deployed
site is unchanged and refuses everything.

Four Owner decisions remain and cannot be inferred by an Agent: the real HTTPS canonical origin; which real authored
Pages may be `published`; whether and when the protected `/` demonstration is replaced or retired; and whether a
rebuild-to-publish operation is approved, since a published address exists only after a build enumerates it.

Proofs a localhost fixture cannot supply remain open: a live HTTPS origin, real browser and accessibility behaviour, and
crawler behaviour. Two engineering dependencies remain: the U04 JSON document contract and renderer before any JSON body
may be served, and the 17-family / 22-module parity obligations.

Three boundaries carry forward: a test-only origin is not a chosen production domain, a fixture publication is not a
delivered website, and a local production build is not a deployment.

That work needs its own Owner-installed work order, its own exact-path ledger and its own independent tests on
both hosts; nothing in this note authorises it.
