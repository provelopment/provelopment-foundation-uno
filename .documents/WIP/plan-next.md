## One unissued candidate

**U02 single-Entity EFS manifest and cardinality refusal (candidate only, not issued).** A
future stage could define one Entity's authored manifest — Facet, Site, Page and localized
representation — with strict refusal of zero-Entity and two-Entity configurations and of
undeclared properties. This candidate is recorded so it is not invented ad hoc later; it
requires its own Owner-installed work order and is **not** part of the current U01 baseline,
which implements no content model, no manifest and no public route.
