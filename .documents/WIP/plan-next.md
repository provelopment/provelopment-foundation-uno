## One unissued candidate

**U03 confined source reading, document/visibility contract and HTTP route integration (candidate
only, not issued).** A later bounded stage could read the authored Markdown/JSON source a validated
candidate address declares, confine that read to the declared content root, parse it into a document,
and only then expose the address through a real Next.js route with metadata, canonical advertising,
`hreflang` and explicit visibility controls. It would also own the safe error behaviour for a
non-matching or invalid pathname and the Owner's decision on any optional base path.

Two hard boundaries belong to that candidate and may not be inferred from the candidate-address
work now complete: a **candidate address is not a served route** until that stage explicitly says so,
and neither the manifest nor a property `public` flag may ever be read as publication authority on
its own. That work needs its own Owner-installed work order, its own exact-path ledger and its own
independent tests; it is not part of the accepted pure candidate-address checkpoint and nothing in
this note authorises it.
