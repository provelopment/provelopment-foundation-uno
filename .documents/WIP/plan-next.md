## One unissued candidate

**Owner live-acceptance decision (candidate only, not issued).** The Vercel production deployment built from this commit
is the first one whose build is warning-free by construction rather than by appearance: the cause of the two whole-project
tracing warnings is removed from the confined reader, the build prints no Next/Turbopack warning or error, and the server
payload contains four project-owned files — the two fixed declarations, the authored Home Markdown and the serving
composition — instead of the entire repository. The published-Page evidence is unchanged and still passes: six Pages
across two authored locales including a custom nested-route Page, over real HTTP and a real headless Chromium DOM, with
crawler parity, fifteen strict refusals, a malformed-JSON non-rescue proof, a keyboard-reachable skip link and a genuine
404 document. The committed deployment still declares no base and publishes no Page, so the live site remains the single
unpublished demonstration.

**What the Owner must judge, and no Agent may decide:**
1. Whether the live `https://uno.provelopment.com/` demonstration is itself acceptable — its copy, its single `h1`, its
   English language, its `noindex`/`nofollow` policy and the absence of a canonical or alternate.
2. Whether the reserved `deployment/site.config.json` presentation authority, and any base-path scope, are assigned to U04
   or a later increment; its absence still means no appearance or tenant configuration exists.
3. The four earlier decisions that remain open: the real HTTPS canonical origin, which named real Pages may publish,
   whether the protected `/` demonstration is retired, and whether rebuild-to-publish is the operational model.
4. Whether U03 may close on this evidence, or must wait for the U04 versioned JSON contract and renderer plus the
   17-family / 22-module parity obligations.

**Not verified here, and not claimed:** the live deployment's own build-warning count and runtime log window (an
independent read-only Vercel observation), a screen-reader audit, real search-engine indexing, production provider
behaviour and real-device browser behaviour.

**Three boundaries carry forward:** a warning-free local build is not a warning-free provider build until read from the
provider; a bounded local payload is not a delivered website; and a `READY` deployment status is not, by itself, proof of
zero warnings.

That work needs its own Owner-installed work order, its own exact-path ledger and its own independent tests on
both hosts; nothing in this note authorises it.
